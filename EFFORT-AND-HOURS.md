# CML / Estra Logics — Effort and Hours Record

## Purpose

This file records the human effort behind CML, Fermata, Estra Logics, the public GitHub repair work, validation experiments, evidence packaging, and related engineering.

The record is intentionally conservative: **hours are not reconstructed from guesses, message counts, commit counts, or CI wall-clock.** Historical work that was not timed contemporaneously is recorded as real work with **unquantified duration** rather than assigned a fabricated number.

2. Do not backfill a number from memory unless it is clearly labeled as an estimate.

4. Preserve failed experiments and HOLD work; they are still engineering effort.
5. Keep human effort separate from AI execution and machine runtime.
6. Never use an hours total as proof of correctness, novelty, or completion.

## Attribution boundary

The engineering record is **human-led and AI-assisted**.

Human effort includes problem selection, direction, acceptance criteria, authorization boundaries, evidence standards, review, corrections, decisions, and final authority.

AI assistance includes drafting, code generation, analysis, test planning, retrieval, and other tool-supported execution under human direction.

The purpose of this record is not to inflate either side. It is to make the work visible without inventing precision that the historical record does not support.
